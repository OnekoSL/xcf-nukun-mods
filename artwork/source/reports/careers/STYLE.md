# Career illustration style

Career images are late-1990s cinematic documentary scenes showing one person in
their ordinary working environment before joining X-Com.

## Shared visual language

- Painterly realism with natural anatomy and practical occupational details.
- Muted earth colors, charcoal, desaturated teal and blue-green.
- Warm practical light mixed with cold night light.
- Quiet unease rather than heroic action.
- One subtle unexplained reflection, shadow or light; never an overt alien.
- No text, lettering, numbers, logos or watermarks inside the illustration.

## Layout

- Generate a 16:10 landscape source with the entire left 52 percent as empty,
  near-black aubergine negative space.
- Keep the person, hands, tools and mystery hint entirely in the right 48 percent.
- All career images are borderless. Never add an outer frame, ornamental edge,
  card outline or hard vertical divider.
- The dark text field must flow softly and continuously into the illustrated
  scene. Preserve a gradual aubergine-to-scene transition instead of a visible
  panel boundary.
- Keep the left field dark and low-detail enough for the existing title and body
  text, but do not turn it into a flat framed rectangle.
- Convert the generated source directly with `convert_cpal.py`, then validate it
  with `audit_cpal.py`.

## Source discovery

- Use the installed X-Com Files career artwork and Ufopaedia rules as the source
  of truth for every remaining career entry.
- Read the German career text before designing its scene so occupation, implied
  history and stat emphasis agree with the illustration.
- User screenshots are optional visual feedback, not required generation inputs.

## Pilot prompts

### Commoner

An ordinary office employee works alone after hours at a cluttered desk with a
beige CRT monitor, paper files, desk phone, coffee cup, coat and shoulder bag.
An indistinct blue-green light or impossible reflection appears in the window
or CRT glass.

### Martial Artist

A martial artist performs an anatomically correct full-body kata in a traditional
dojo, wearing an off-white gi and dark belt. A paper screen or practice mirror
shows a subtle elongated nonhuman-looking shadow that could still be a trick of
the light.

## Batch 02 motif briefs

### Saboteur

A covert operative disables an industrial control cabinet after hours. Tools,
wiring and hand placement remain plausible; one unfamiliar cyan component in
the cabinet provides the restrained mystery note.

### Ranger

A rain-soaked ranger scouts a guarded installation from a forested rise using
a rifle, map and field radio. Two faint hovering lights beyond the compound are
ambiguous enough to be mistaken for aircraft or reflections.

### Sapper

An explosives expert kneels at a breached wall in an abandoned military or
industrial facility and prepares a controlled shaped charge. A seamless cold
glow behind the broken concrete suggests an unexpected buried material.

### Marine

An elite assault veteran advances through a freshly breached compound with a
rifle held at low ready. A distant cold silhouette in the corridor is the only
unexplained element; the scene remains grounded in late-1990s equipment.

## Representation across the series

- Balance women and men across each group of career illustrations instead of
  allowing the original artwork to default mostly to men.
- When a career text does not specify gender, casting may be chosen freely.
- Keep every character occupationally credible; diversity must not replace the
  practical clothing, anatomy, tools and actions required by the career.

## Batch 03 motif briefs

### Spy

A female intelligence operative searches a secure late-1990s records room and
discovers an impossible blue-green geometric detail on photographic film.

### Marksman

A male military marksman observes a damaged industrial compound from concealed
high ground at dusk. An angular distant shadow is the restrained mystery clue.

### Soldier

A female military officer pauses at a jungle checkpoint after finding scorched,
nonhuman-looking tracks and a seamless metallic fragment near the perimeter.

### Police Officer

A male plainclothes detective examines an abandoned cult meeting site in a wet
urban alley. A distorted silhouette in the wall reflection does not match any
person present.

## Batch 04 motif briefs

### Detective

A female private detective compares surveillance photographs and a street map
in a shabby rented office. The same distant figure appears in several images.

### Journalist

A female investigative journalist examines freshly developed photographs in a
newspaper archive and darkroom. One apparent lens flare defies explanation.

### Student

A male university student uses homemade late-1990s laboratory equipment to
measure a repeating signal and tries to rationalize the anomalous waveform.

### Sailor

A female deckhand braces on a working vessel during a cold night storm while a
vast diffuse blue-green light moves beneath the sea against the current.

## Batch 05 motif briefs

### Mystery Hunter

A female field explorer documents an ancient ruin at night while the shadow on
a stone wall implies a many-jointed creature that is not otherwise visible.

### Pilot

A male rescue helicopter pilot crosses a storm in an analog late-1990s cockpit.
One silent blue-green light holds position against the wind ahead of him.

### Medic

A female emergency medic studies a radiograph after a night shift. A tiny
seamless geometric object appears where no ordinary implant should exist.

### Psychologist

A male clinical psychologist reviews a completed session on cassette after
hours. A second repeating pulse appears on a recording that should be silent.

## Batch 06 motif briefs

### Fireman

A female firefighter advances through a smoke-filled damaged building with a
hose and thermal camera. A human-shaped region is inexplicably cold.

### Explorer

A male high-altitude explorer documents an ancient structure exposed by a
glacier and a small perfectly smooth object embedded beneath old ice.

### Data Analyst

A female data analyst compares unrelated late-1990s data streams and discovers
the same impossibly precise geometric cluster repeating across them.

### Adept

A female adept performs a restrained parapsychology test with analog sensors
and face-down unmarked cards while a tuning fork barely lifts from the table.

## Batch 07 motif briefs

### Thief

A female thief quietly bypasses the window lock of a records office after
midnight. An impossible second silhouette appears only in the wet reflection.

### Illusionist

A male stage illusionist rehearses a precise coin manipulation backstage in a
worn late-1990s theatre. His mirror image subtly disagrees with the real pose.

### Delivery Driver

A female night courier checks a paper manifest beside an unbranded delivery
van at a rain-soaked loading dock. Cold light leaks from one sealed parcel.

### Mountaineer

A female mountaineer climbs a technically credible mixed rock-and-ice route at
dusk. Unfamiliar footprints cross an otherwise inaccessible snow shelf above.

## Batch 08 motif briefs

### Driver

A male long-haul driver pauses on a rain-soaked road at night with an analog
truck cab, paper map and thermos. Three unexplained lights fill the windshield.

### Athlete

A female javelin athlete completes a technically credible throw in an empty,
wet stadium after dusk. Her cast shadow disagrees with the stadium lighting.

### Laborer

A female crane operator works an old analog harbor crane during a rainy night
shift. A source-less blue-green glow hangs over the distant container yard.

### Fencer

A male sport fencer removes his mask after practice in a worn fencing salle.
The wall mirror retains the shadow of a second opponent in the empty room.

## Batch 09 motif briefs

### Mystic

A female mystic meditates in a sparse stone retreat with beads, candle and an
analog timer. The flame and incense smoke bend in contradictory directions.

### Engineer

A male mechanical engineer inspects a hazardous old turbine during a night
shift. A repeating blue-green frost lattice covers part of the hot housing.

### Astronomer

A female astronomer compares a photographic plate and paper chart in an analog
observatory. The same impossible triangle appears on the plate and live sky.

### Trader

A female wholesale trader closes her market warehouse using a ledger, scale
and cash box. A seamless dark object among ordinary goods casts no shadow.

## Batch 10 motif briefs

### Punk

A male street punk repairs a battered cassette player in a wet service alley.
A tall absent figure appears only in the puddle reflection beside him.

### Coach

A female personal mentor listens to a troubled client in a modest late-1990s
community room. The wall carries a second shadow behind the seated client.

### Farmer

An older male farmer repairs a damaged livestock fence before dawn. A perfect
circle of frost surrounds one post while the rest of the ground remains wet.

### Hunter

A female hunter examines tracks beside a cold forest stream with her rifle
safely slung. Deer prints become deep three-pronged impressions, then stop.

## Batch 11 motif briefs

### Priest

A female clergy member tends a votive stand in a quiet chapel after hours.
The soot above one extinguished candle forms a precise human-shaped void.

### Historian

An older male historian compares a folded map and archival photograph under a
magnifying glass. Both reveal the same faint geometric palimpsest.

### Entertainer

A female singer and host waits backstage with a microphone and cassette
recorder. Her dressing-room mirror shows an audience in the empty hall.

### Negotiator

A male negotiator mediates between two tense representatives at a late-night
table. A stopped cassette recorder still shows moving level needles.

## Batch 12 motif briefs

### Astronaut

A female astronaut compares analog mission photographs inside a late-1990s
space-agency preparation room. The same triangular light appears in a print
and in the reflection of a powered-off monitor.

### Institutionalized

A dignified male former psychiatric patient calmly sorts repeated drawings in
a therapeutic observation room. Their geometric figure reappears as a shadow
behind the empty observation glass.

### Mercenary

An experienced female mercenary inspects recovered evidence in an improvised
industrial safehouse. A fused glass fragment casts a sharp three-pronged
shadow in the wrong direction.

### Bodyguard

A male bodyguard shields a dignitary beside a waiting sedan in a hotel service
area. An analog security monitor shows a tall figure on the visibly empty
escape route.

## Final career motif briefs

### Aristocrat

A well-connected female aristocrat discreetly examines a sealed archival
folder after a private diplomatic reception. A mirror retains the reflection
of one formal guest who is absent from the room.

### Secret Agent

A female covert operative changes film in an analog camera inside a rainy
late-1990s surveillance safehouse. One developed photograph shows a tall
figure directly behind her although the room is empty.
